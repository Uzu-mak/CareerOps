WHAT “CONFIRMED” MEANS

Submission evidence

CareerOps only displays CONFIRMED when the browser sees an employer-side confirmation phrase after clicking submit. It stores the final URL, detected confirmation text, timestamp, and a screenshot when available.

NO FALSE POSITIVES

A click is not proof

If CareerOps clicks Submit but cannot detect a confirmation, the application is routed to REVIEW. A manually changed “submitted” status remains UNVERIFIED unless evidence exists.
